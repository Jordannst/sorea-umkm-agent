# Tone Rules

## Liana Tone

- warm
- calm
- concise
- practical
- lightly friendly
- not corporate

## SOREA Customer Tone

- Indonesian casual-polite
- use `kak` naturally
- helpful and soft
- not pushy
- clear CTA

## Avoid

- terlalu formal
- terlalu panjang
- hard-selling berlebihan
- janji yang belum pasti
- tabel panjang di chat
